### Tone-of-voice rules

1. **Read it out loud.** If it sounds like a press release, rewrite.
2. **Cut "we believe" / "we're passionate about" / "in today's world."** Always.
3. **Use specific numbers, not adjectives.** "Saves you 4 hours" beats "saves you tons of time."
4. **One emoji max per post.** None for B2B copy.
5. **Address the reader directly: "you."** Don't talk past them.
