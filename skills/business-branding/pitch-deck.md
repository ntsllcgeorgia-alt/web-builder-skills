# Pitch Deck Template (10 slides)

The structure used by YC, a16z, Sequoia portfolio companies. Hand this to Claude and say "fill it in for {{my idea}}."

---

## Slide 1 — Title

- **Company name** (large)
- **One-line tagline** ("X for Y" works: "Stripe for healthcare")
- **Founder name + email**
- **Date**

---

## Slide 2 — The Problem

- Who has it (specific persona)
- How painful (use a number — hours, dollars, frustration count)
- Why it's not solved today
- **Format:** 3 bullets, NOT paragraphs.

---

## Slide 3 — The Solution

- One sentence: what you built
- One screenshot or product diagram (visual is critical)
- 3 bullets on key capability
- **The "aha":** what's unique that competitors don't have

---

## Slide 4 — Why Now

- What changed in the world that makes this possible / necessary now
- Tech enabler (AI, mobile, regulation, etc.)
- Market shift or behavior change
- **Format:** "5 years ago this couldn't exist because X. Now it can because Y."

---

## Slide 5 — Market Size

- TAM / SAM / SOM with specific dollar figures + source
- Show it visually (3 nested circles or boxes)
- **Avoid:** "$X trillion industry" if your slice is tiny

---

## Slide 6 — The Product

- 2-3 product screenshots
- Annotated with what each screen does
- Show the *outcome* the user gets, not just the UI

---

## Slide 7 — Traction (or "Validation" if pre-revenue)

- Numbers, in big bold type:
  - {{X}} customers / users / pilots
  - ${{X}} ARR / MRR
  - {{X}}% MoM growth
- If pre-revenue: pre-orders, waitlist, LOIs, design partners
- **A line chart up and to the right** is the highest-leverage visual in any pitch deck

---

## Slide 8 — Business Model

- How you charge (subscription / transactional / freemium)
- Unit economics in one table:
  - ARPU (avg revenue per user)
  - CAC (customer acquisition cost)
  - LTV
  - Gross margin
- Path to profitability — at what scale

---

## Slide 9 — Competition

- 2x2 chart with your brand top-right
- X-axis and Y-axis are the two dimensions you win on
- Logos of competitors plotted on the other 3 quadrants
- One-sentence why you win below the chart

---

## Slide 10 — The Team

- Photos + names + 1-line credential each
- Why THIS team will win (relevant background, prior wins, unique insight)
- Open roles you're hiring

---

## Slide 11 (optional) — The Ask

- Raising ${{amount}}
- Use of funds (3 buckets, % each)
- Milestones the round funds (next 18 months)
- Lead investor / committed amounts (if any)

---

## Design rules

- **Black or white background.** No gradient noise.
- **One idea per slide.** If a slide has two ideas, split it.
- **Type ≥24pt.** Investors read on a screen across a table.
- **Numbers are huge.** "$2.4M ARR" gets its own slide if needed.
- **Logos > photos > illustrations > clip art.** Skip the AI-generated stock images.
- **No emoji on title slides.** Looks unserious.

## Delivery rules

- 10 minutes max. 1 minute per slide.
- Memorize first and last 30 seconds. Wing the middle.
- End with a clear ask. Don't trail off.
