# Cold Email Sequences

Proven outreach formats that actually get replies. Use these as starting points — fill in the specifics.

## Universal rules

1. **Subject lines under 6 words.** "{first_name} — quick question" beats anything corporate.
2. **First line must NOT be about you.** Reference something specific they did.
3. **Total email under 80 words.** Anything longer gets skimmed or ignored.
4. **One CTA.** A 15-min call. A reply. Never two.
5. **Never:** "I hope this finds you well", "I help X do Y", "circle back", "synergy".

---

## Sequence 1: Sales outreach (5 emails over 3 weeks)

### Email 1 — Day 0

> **Subject:** {{first_name}} — quick one
>
> Hey {{first_name}} —
>
> Saw {{specific thing they did or shipped}}. Sharp move.
>
> Quick one: we help {{their type of company}} {{specific outcome with a number — "cut SDR ramp time from 90 days to 30"}}.
>
> Worth a 15-min call this week?
>
> {{your name}}

### Email 2 — Day 3 (reply to Email 1)

> Bumping this — {{specific reason they'd care, like "saw you just hired a new VP Sales, this might be useful timing"}}.
>
> Open to it?

### Email 3 — Day 8 (new thread, different angle)

> **Subject:** {{benefit angle}} — for {{their company}}
>
> {{First name}} — different angle.
>
> {{Customer logo or proof point}} just {{result}} using us. Same setup as {{their company}}: {{shared trait}}.
>
> Want the 1-pager?

### Email 4 — Day 14 (give-then-ask)

> **Subject:** something useful for {{their company}}
>
> Made a quick teardown of {{relevant thing — their funnel, their landing page, their pricing page}}. 3 specific suggestions.
>
> Want me to send it? No pitch attached.

### Email 5 — Day 21 (break-up)

> **Subject:** closing the loop
>
> Sounds like the timing isn't right — totally fine.
>
> If anything changes, my line is open. And if you're never the right fit, just reply "stop" and I'll never bug you again.

## Sequence 2: Partnership outreach (3 emails)

### Email 1

> **Subject:** {{their brand}} + {{your brand}} — possible collab?
>
> Hey {{first_name}},
>
> {{Your company}} works with {{shared customer type}}. We've been looking at brands that nail {{shared value}} — yours is in the top 3 we keep mentioning.
>
> Quick 15-min to explore a co-marketing or referral angle?
>
> {{your name}}

### Email 2 — Day 5

> Bumping. We just {{recent proof/launch/win}} — it'd be a strong story together with what you're doing.

### Email 3 — Day 14 (concrete offer)

> Putting a specific idea on the table:
>
> - {{specific ask}}
> - In exchange: {{specific give}}
>
> If this is interesting, 15 min next week?

## Sequence 3: Hiring outreach (recruiter or founder direct)

### Email 1

> **Subject:** {{role}} at {{your company}} — you came up
>
> Hey {{first_name}},
>
> Came across your work at {{their employer}} — specifically {{specific project/post/repo}}. We're hiring a {{role}} and your background lines up almost exactly.
>
> What we're building: {{1 sentence}}
> Comp range: ${{X}}K - ${{Y}}K + equity
> Stage: {{seed/Series A/etc.}}, {{# people}} on the team
>
> Worth 20 min to compare notes? Worst case, you get coffee money for an interesting convo.

### Email 2 — Day 4

> Quick bump — wanted to add: we're {{remote / hybrid / Atlanta-based / wherever}}, and the role would have you {{1-line of unique scope}}.
>
> Yes / no / "not now"?

### Email 3 — Day 10

> All good — just closing the loop. If you know anyone strong, intros are gold and I'd return the favor.

### Email 1

> **Subject:** {{their company}} — checking in
>
> Hey {{first_name}},
>
> We talked back in {{month}}. You said {{their specific blocker — "we want to wait until Q3 budget" or "we're focused on the redesign first"}}.
>
> {{Whatever they were waiting for}} should be wrapping up soon. Worth picking it back up?

## Personalization triggers (what to reference in line 1)

In priority order — use the highest one available:

1. **A recent post, podcast, or talk they gave** ("Saw your talk at SaaStr — the bit on customer expansion was sharp.")
2. **A recent funding round or product launch** ("Saw the Series A — congrats.")
3. **A new hire on their team in a related role** ("Noticed you brought on {{name}} as VP Sales — congrats. Reaching out because...")
4. **Their company's recent press / news**
5. **A specific public thing they did** (open-source repo, blog post, course)
6. **Mutual connection** ("{{Name}} suggested I reach out.")

If none of the above are true: don't send the email. Find a real hook or skip them.

---

## Reply-rate benchmarks

- **Generic outreach:** 1-3% reply rate
- **Personalized first line:** 8-15%
- **Multi-touch sequence (5 emails):** 15-25% total reply rate
- **Hyper-targeted with mutual connection:** 30%+

If you're getting <5% across a sequence, the problem is the targeting (wrong person) or the offer (not relevant), not the copy.
