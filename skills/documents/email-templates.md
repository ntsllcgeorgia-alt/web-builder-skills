# Business Email Templates

Common situations, ready-to-send formats. Claude fills in the brackets.

## Proposal follow-up (after sending a quote)

> **Subject:** {{project}} — quick check
>
> Hey {{first_name}},
>
> Wanted to follow up on the proposal I sent {{day, e.g. "Tuesday"}}.
>
> Any questions on it? Happy to jump on a 10-min call to walk through anything that needs clarifying.
>
> Otherwise, just let me know if you're a yes, no, or "need more time" — any answer is fine.
>
> {{your name}}

## Missed meeting (no-show)

> **Subject:** missed connection
>
> Hey {{first_name}} —
>
> Looks like we got crossed wires on the {{time}} call. Things come up — no worries.
>
> Want to reschedule? Here's my calendar: {{cal link}}
>
> Or if the timing isn't right anymore, just say so and we'll close the loop.

## Refund / credit request from a customer

> **Subject:** Re: {{their subject}}
>
> Hey {{first_name}},
>
> Thanks for flagging this — and sorry for the friction.
>
> I've issued the {{refund / credit}} of ${{amount}}. It should hit your account in {{3-5 business days for cards, 1-2 for bank}}.
>
> One quick thing: {{specific question to learn what went wrong, e.g. "What was the issue with the {{product}}? I want to make sure we don't repeat it."}}
>
> {{your name}}

## Asking for a testimonial

> **Subject:** would mean a lot
>
> Hey {{first_name}},
>
> Working on the website refresh and would love to feature {{their company}} as a customer story.
>
> Could you spare 5 minutes to send 2-3 sentences on what we've helped you do? Specifically:
>
> 1. What problem were you trying to solve?
> 2. What's changed since?
>
> If easier, jump on a 10-min call and I'll write it up + send for your approval.
>
> Thanks for considering.

## Termination of a service / contractor

> **Subject:** Re: {{contract / service}}
>
> Hey {{first_name}},
>
> Wanted to give you a heads up directly: we've decided to end the {{contract / engagement}} effective {{date — usually 30 days out}}.
>
> The work has been good — this is more about {{honest reason: budget, internal change, scope no longer matches}}, not a reflection on what you delivered.
>
> Let's set up a 15-min call this week to handle handoff, final invoice, and any cleanup.
>
> Thanks for the work.

---

## General rules

1. **Subject line: under 6 words, no caps lock.**
2. **First line: not about you.** "Hope you're well" is OK only if you've worked with them before.
3. **Bold the ask** if there is one. Make it impossible to miss.
4. **Sign off with a question if you want a reply.** People respond to questions, not statements.
5. **No "Sent from my iPhone."** Even if it's true, set a real signature.
